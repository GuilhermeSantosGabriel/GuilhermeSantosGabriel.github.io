### What i did

This tutorial was more of a presentation of different concepts on the character device drivers. First we need to understand what they are:

Character devices try to abstract different types of devices that work with characters, like keyboards or a mouse so that their output look like files and not streams of data. They are represented in linux by "two" numbers (major and minor) that under the hood are only one. They need to support file operations like open, close read and write. With that in mind i implemented the "simple_char" character device which only loads a character device that looks like a real one.
